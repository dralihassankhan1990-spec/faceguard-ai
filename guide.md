# FaceGuard AI User Guide

Welcome to FaceGuard AI. This application uses advanced AI vision to detect faces and manage attendance automatically.

## 1. Getting Started
When you open the app, ensure you allow **Camera Permissions** in your browser when prompted.

## 2. Modes

### A. Detector Mode (Surveillance)
*   **Purpose:** Logs any human face that appears in front of the camera.
*   **How to use:**
    1.  Click "Detector" in the top navigation.
    2.  Click the Camera icon to start.
    3.  The system will continuously scan. When a face is seen, it captures a photo, logs the timestamp, and speaks a confirmation.
    4.  View the history in the "Live Logs" panel on the right.

### B. Attendance Mode
*   **Purpose:** Mark attendance for specific registered users.
*   **Step 1: Registration**
    1.  Switch to "Attendance" mode.
    2.  Click the **"Register"** button (Purple).
    3.  Enter the user's Full Name.
    4.  Turn on the camera.
    5.  Ensure the face is clearly visible and click **"Capture & Save"**.
*   **Step 2: Auto-Marking**
    1.  Click the **"Auto Mark"** button (Blue).
    2.  Turn on the camera.
    3.  The system enters "Hands-Free" mode.
    4.  Any registered user who walks in front of the camera will be automatically identified.
    5.  Their attendance is marked, and a voice welcome message is played.
    6.  **Note:** To prevent spam, the system waits 60 seconds before marking the same person again.

## Tips for Performance
*   **Lighting:** Ensure good lighting on the face for faster recognition.
*   **Distance:** Stand within 1-2 meters of the camera.
*   **Privacy:** All images are stored locally in your browser's storage. Clearing browser cache will remove registered users.
